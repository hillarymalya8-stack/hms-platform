BOOKINGS PAID BEFORE CHECK-IN

What this version already supports
1. Keep the booking confirmed until the guest actually arrives.
2. At check-in choose "Yes - paid before arrival (company or guest)".
3. Choose the meal plan, enter the actual amount already received in the stay's
   currency, add the company if applicable and enter the payment reference.
4. The system adds this amount as a deposit credit to the guest folio.
5. Room and POS charges use that credit; collect any remaining balance at checkout.
6. Do not collect or enter the same advance payment again at checkout.

Example: Room charges 200,000 plus extras 30,000, with an advance payment of
150,000, leaves 80,000 to collect. Use one currency throughout this example.

Important limits of the current workflow
- The advance credit is entered at check-in, rather than on the day payment arrives.
- It creates a folio deposit and audit entry; it does not create a Payment record
  or a separately numbered payment receipt for the original receipt date.
- Reservation invoices currently show selected charges; their total does not
  subtract deposits or settlement payments. Use the guest folio for the amount due.
- The check-in form requires a meal plan for prepaid bookings. It has no room-only
  prepaid option. Select the package actually agreed with the guest.

Recommended next improvement (not implemented in this update)
Add a "Record advance payment" action on confirmed reservations. Record the
actual payment date, amount, currency, payment method, transaction reference,
payer and a unique receipt number at the time money arrives. Show a receipt
and payment history on the booking. Carry the existing credit into check-in
automatically so reception cannot enter it twice. Show charges, payments and
remaining balance separately on the final invoice. Handle cancellations,
refunds and unused credit through an explicit recorded action.
